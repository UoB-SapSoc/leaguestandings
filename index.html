    Elo System and Points Rules

    First, what is an elo rating system? An elo rating system is a method for calculating the relative skill level of players or teams. Originally designed by Arpad Elo, it has been used in many popular games and sports suchas, tennis, chess and basketball.

    How does it work? The elo rating of two players serves as a predictor of the outcome of the game. Players with equal rating are expected to win an equal number of games given enough time. If one player is 100 elo above the other they are expected to win 59% of the games, 200 above should win 68% of the time. At the end of every game the winning player takes elo from the loser. The amount exchanged depends on the difference in rating between the players. If the higher rated player wins only a small amount of points will be taken from the loser; however if the lower rated player wins more elo points will be transfered. All this means that given enough games played each elo rating will correct to match the players relative skill compared to the other players in the league.

    Specific methods used in this elo system. I have implimented a quick rank system, as every players starts on 1000 elo, this method aims to more quickly find the players skill level. When a new player joins the league, for the first several games the change in the players elo is multiplied after a match resolves. The multiplication factor follows the graph where total games played is on the x-axis and multiplication factor is on the y-axis. In summary, the elo gained and lost decays exponetially from 3x to 1x between 0 and 16 games played. I also implimented an elo decay mechanic which begins to remove elo from players if they are absent for 3 or more sessions. It gradually increases the amount lost after each session not attended. The amount decayed follows the pattern; 3 sessions missed in a row -5 elo at the end of the session, 4 missed -10 elo, 5 -15, 6 -25, 7 or more missed -50 elo. The limit of elo decay is 800. Also, after 5 sessions absent in a row the player becomes inactive and is marked that way on the leaderboard.

    How do points work? When a match is resolved the winner takes 1 point by default and the losers points always remain the same. this is added to the players total. If the winner of the match was on 10 or more points below the loser before the match the winner is awarded another 20% of the losers total points on top of 1 point by default. Importantly, at the end of each semester the points reset and a semester champion is crowed as the player with the most points in that semester.
